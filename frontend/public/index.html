About KenyaWatch AI
Open-source AI platform for anti-corruption in Kenya
HACKATHON 2026
🎯 The Problem
Corruption costs Kenya an estimated KES 500 billion annually — roughly 30% of the national budget. Public procurement accounts for 60% of government spending, making it the single greatest corruption vulnerability. Manual audit systems cannot keep pace with modern financial fraud.
💡 Our Solution
KenyaWatch AI combines ML fraud detection, satellite image verification, and citizen reporting into a single real-time intelligence platform — empowering oversight bodies, journalists, and citizens to identify and act on corruption at scale.
🏗 Tech Stack
BACKEND
Node.js + Express on Railway. PostgreSQL database. Anthropic Claude API for AI chat. Rate limiting & helmet security.
FRONTEND
Vanilla HTML/CSS/JS served via Express on Railway. Calls backend API. Zero frameworks — fast, lightweight, deployable anywhere.
DATA
Kenya e-GP, PPIP portal, KRA iTax registry, Sentinel-2 satellite API, EACC public database & citizen reports.
